# Claim Boundary

## Established conditionally

If a refinement family has an even-power expansion, the displayed two-, three-, and four-scale weights cancel the first one, two, and three error modes exactly.

## Supported numerically

The tested regular cap, spherical-triangle, latitude-longitude, and irregular-polygon families exhibit observed orders near 2, 4, 6, and 8.

## Conjectural

A general even-power area law for all regular, scale-consistent spherical refinement families is not proved here.

## Not claimed

- a universal correction for arbitrary meshes;
- a new extrapolation algorithm;
- stable sixth- or eighth-order behavior for the perturbed grids;
- validity for nonconvex, self-intersecting, adaptive, or geometrically ill-posed refinements.
